---
title: Installing the app
description: Add Open Chat Interface to your phone's home screen or your computer's desktop, what an installed copy is, and why it has no offline mode.
---

You can add Open Chat Interface (OCI) to your phone's home screen or your computer's desktop. It then opens in its own window, without the browser's address bar and tabs, with an icon, a name and colours chosen by your institution.

An installed OCI is the same website, not a separate program. Everything works as it does in the browser, and updates arrive the way they do for any web page: the next time you open it.

## It needs a network connection

OCI has no offline mode. Your conversations are kept on the server, so a copy on your device would be out of date, and it would be a copy of your private conversations left on a phone. With no connection the installed app shows the same error page your browser would show for any site, and nothing from OCI is kept to show instead. Open it again once you are back online.

## How to install

On **Settings → Customization**, the **Install the app** card at the bottom of the page has an **Install** button. Select it and follow your browser's prompt.

The button appears only while your browser has said it can install the app. If you have already installed it, or your browser cannot, the button is not shown. Safari never says so, which is why iPhones, iPads and Macs use the steps in this table instead.

| Where | How |
|---|---|
| Chrome or Edge on Android | **Settings → Customization → Install**, or the browser menu → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**). |
| Chrome or Edge on a computer | **Settings → Customization → Install**, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar. |
| Safari on iPhone or iPad | **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**. |
| Safari on a Mac | **File** → **Add to Dock**. |
| Firefox | Firefox on a computer cannot install websites. On Android, the menu → **Install**. |

## Good to know

- **Name and icon.** They are your instance's name, short name and logo, as your administrator set them. Under the icon, phones show the short name, cut to 12 characters. Android and Chrome notice a change when they next check. On an iPhone or iPad the name and icon are copied when you add the app, so remove it and add it again to pick up a change.
- **Signing in.** An app added on an iPhone or iPad may keep its own sign-in, separate from Safari's, so you may need to sign in once there.
- **Removing it.** Press and hold the icon, or use the app's own menu, and choose to remove or uninstall it. Your conversations are not affected.
