## What your institution decides

Several things you might expect to set yourself are decided for the whole instance, often per role:

- **Which models appear** in the picker.
- **How much you may use**, in messages, tokens or cost.
- **How long conversations are kept** before they are removed.
- **Which features you have**: web search, attachments, share links, temporary chats, branching (editing and forking), projects, memory and artifacts.
- **Which tools models may use for you**, and how many steps a reply may take.
- **Which reasoning levels you can choose**, and which one a new conversation starts at.

If something described here is missing for you, it has been turned off rather than broken. A refused request says it is "not available for your role". Ask whoever runs your instance.

## On a phone

OCI works on a phone. The sidebar becomes a panel you open from the top left, the message box's controls collapse behind one button, and artifacts open full screen.
