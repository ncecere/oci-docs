title: Sharing
description: Publish a read-only link to a conversation, live or as a fixed snapshot, with an optional expiry, and revoke it.

Sharing publishes a read-only copy of a conversation at a link anybody can open, without an account.

## Creating a link

Open the conversation and select **Share** (at the top right). In the **Share conversation** dialog:

- **Share through**: **Latest messages (live)** shows the conversation as it grows, including messages you add later. Choose one of the messages instead for a **fixed snapshot** that ends at that message and never changes.
- **Expires (optional)**: a date and time, in your local time, after which the link stops working. Blank means no expiry.

Select **Create and copy link**. The link is on your clipboard.

You can have several links for one conversation; each is independent, and creating a new one does not revoke the others. **Existing links** in the same dialog lists them, with **Copy** and **Revoke**.

## What is included

- The messages, up to the point you chose, with the reply shown for each question and which model wrote it.
- [Artifacts](/docs/using/artifacts) of the shared replies, at the version current for them, in a sealed frame.
- Tool steps as one-line summaries.

Not included:

- **Reasoning**: the thinking a model did on the way to an answer.
- **Attachments**: the shared page shows the messages without them.
- **Project instructions and files**.
- **Anything about your account** beyond a display name.

## Revoking

**Revoke** makes a link stop working at once. Anybody who opened it before keeps whatever they saved or copied.

Deleting the conversation, by you or by retention, also revokes its links, and restoring it from the trash does **not** bring them back: create a new link. Links to a temporary chat stop working when it expires.

## Before you share

The link needs no account, so treat it as public. Your institution can turn sharing off for everyone or for your role; if **Share** is missing, that is why.
