## The sidebar

The sidebar lists your [projects](/docs/using/projects), each with its own conversations, then your other conversations under **Pinned**, **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Older**.

<Screenshot slot="sidebar-projects" />

- Under **Projects**, each project has an arrow that shows or hides its five most recent conversations; select its name to open the project page. **Show all (*n*)** opens the project's conversations. Projects start collapsed, and whether each is open is remembered in this browser. The project you are working in opens by itself while you are there.
- A conversation in a project is listed under its project, not in the date groups.
- **Pinned** lists every pinned conversation, including project ones, which show their project's name after the title.

On a phone, open the sidebar from the top left.

## Pinning and archiving

Point at a conversation in the sidebar (or move to it with Tab) to show its buttons:

- **Pin** keeps it at the top, under **Pinned**. **Unpin** puts it back.
- **Archive** removes it from the sidebar without deleting it. Archived conversations are under [Settings → History](/docs/using/settings#history) → **Archived**, where **Restore** brings one back. Search still finds them.

Titles are set automatically from your first message.

## The trash

Delete conversations from [Settings → History](/docs/using/settings#history): tick them and select **Delete**. They move to the **Trash** tab, where each shows when it will be deleted for good.

- **Restore** brings a conversation back (with its files, if you have room for them in your storage allowance). Share links it had stay revoked.
- **Delete now** deletes one permanently; **Empty trash** deletes them all. This cannot be undone.

How long the trash keeps things, and whether old conversations are moved there automatically, is set by your institution. A conversation removed for inactivity is marked "removed automatically".

## Finding a conversation

Type in the sidebar's search box, or press `Cmd/Ctrl + K`, to search your conversations. Results show each conversation's title with its best-matching lines underneath, matched words highlighted, best match first.

<Screenshot slot="conversation-search" />

- Every word you type must appear, and each matches the start of a word: `migr plan` finds "migration planning". Punctuation is ignored.
- Search covers titles, your messages and the replies you were shown. It does not search reasoning, web sources or the contents of attached files.
- Archived conversations are included and marked **Archived**; project conversations show their project. Conversations in the trash and temporary chats are not included.
- Choosing a result opens the conversation at the matching message, briefly highlighted, instead of at the end. **Jump to latest** takes you to the end.

`Cmd/Ctrl + K` also opens commands: a new chat, your history, models, attachments, settings, switching theme, and (for administrators and auditors) every admin page.

## Downloading one conversation

The download button at the top right of a conversation saves it as Markdown. To save everything at once, see [Your data](/docs/using/your-data).

## Temporary chats

A temporary chat stays out of the sidebar and search, and expires. See [Chatting](/docs/using/chat#temporary-chats).
