## Acceptable use policy

**Sign-in & security → Acceptable use** (`/admin/policies`). A policy people must accept before they can use the instance. It cannot be dismissed.

<Screenshot slot="admin-acceptable-use" />

### Versioned, never edited

Publishing a change creates a new version. An acceptance records agreement to specific words, so rewriting the text under an existing acceptance would make the record untrue.

- **Publishing a new version asks everybody again**, automatically, and tells them the policy changed rather than showing it as though it were new.
- **A version somebody accepted cannot be deleted.** The database refuses.
- **What is recorded**: the version, the moment and the address it came from. Enough to answer "what exactly did this person agree to, and when".

### Advice

Publish before opening the instance: a policy published later prompts everybody at once, which looks like an incident to people who have been working happily for months. Say in it which providers people's messages and files go to, how long conversations are kept ([Retention](/docs/administration/retention)), and whether conversation content is exported ([Compliance](/docs/administration/compliance)).

## Announcements

**Appearance & features → Announcements** (`/admin/broadcasts`). A banner shown to everybody, or to chosen roles: planned maintenance, a policy change, an incident.

<Screenshot slot="admin-announcements" />

- **A banner, not a toast**, so a maintenance notice stays readable.
- **Dismissal is per person.** One person hiding it does not hide it for others.
- **Editing does not show it again**, so fixing a typo does not interrupt people who read it. **Re-show** clears dismissals when a change matters.
- **Non-dismissable** is enforced by the server, not by hiding the button.
- **Audience** can be limited by role, so a message for staff can skip students.
