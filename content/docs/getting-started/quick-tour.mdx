A five-minute walk through OCI, first as someone using it, then as an administrator.

## Signing in

You sign in with your institution's identity provider (a button such as "Continue with Example University SSO"), with an email and password, or both, depending on how the instance is set up. If your institution publishes an acceptable use policy, you accept it first.

<Screenshot slot="sign-in" />

A new account is greeted by a short introduction: your name, what you do and how you like replies written. It feeds the instructions sent with your messages; **Skip for now** dismisses it, and everything can be set later in [Settings → Customization](/docs/using/settings#customization).

## The parts of the screen

- **The sidebar**, on the left: **New Chat**, search, your **projects**, each with its recent conversations, then your other conversations under **Pinned**, **Today**, **Yesterday** and **Older**. Your name at the bottom opens a menu with **Settings**, **Sign out** and, for administrators, **Admin dashboard**.
- **The message box** at the bottom: type and press Enter. Along its edge: the **model** picker, the **reasoning level** when the model offers one, **Search** for web search, and **Attach**.
- **The buttons at the top right** of a conversation: download it as Markdown, **Share**, **Move to project**, summarise earlier messages, **Temporary chat**, and the appearance menu.

<Screenshot slot="chat-home" />

On a phone the sidebar becomes a panel you open from the top left, and the message box's controls collapse behind one button.

## A first conversation

<Steps>
<Step>

### Pick a model

Select the model name in the message box. Coloured icons show what each model can do; the funnel filters by ability, and **i** opens a model's card. See [Chatting](/docs/using/chat#choosing-a-model).

</Step>
<Step>

### Ask

Type a question and press Enter. The reply streams in; the stop button, where Send was, ends it early. A reasoning model shows **Thinking…** with its latest lines while it works.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep going

Ask a follow-up, switch model mid-conversation, **Retry** the latest reply, or **Fork conversation here** to try a different direction without losing what you have. The conversation gets a title on its own and appears in the sidebar.

</Step>
<Step>

### Make something

Ask for a web page, a diagram or a long document, and it opens as an [artifact](/docs/using/artifacts) beside the conversation, where you can preview, copy, download or export it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Administration

Administrators open **Admin dashboard** from the menu under their name; administrators and auditors can also reach it, and each admin page, from the command palette (`Cmd/Ctrl + K`) or at `/admin`. The dashboard is grouped by task: **People**, **Models**, **Tools & integrations**, **Sign-in & security**, **Data & storage**, **Insights** and **Appearance & features**, with **Overview** above them.

**Overview** opens with a setup checklist: what is still missing before the instance works as intended, in the order things depend on each other, each item linking to the page that resolves it. On a fresh install, start there. See [First run](/docs/administration/first-run).

<Screenshot slot="admin-overview" />

An `auditor` sees the same pages with a read-only banner, and controls that would change something are hidden or disabled.
